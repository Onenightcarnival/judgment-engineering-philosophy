# Those Who Get It Need No Teaching; Those Who Need Teaching Won't Get It

Whether anything is passed on depends on how close the receiver already is.

## Three bands

| Receiver | What it needs | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Already gets it | Nothing | No teaching needed |
| One step away | One sentence, one example | Gets it |
| Far away | No amount of explaining is enough | Does not get it |

Teaching works only in the middle band.

## What sets the distance

| | A person | An agent |
|---|---|---|
| What sets the distance | What has been accumulated, plus initiative | The model's capability |
| Can it be taught | Accumulation yes, initiative no | No; only a different model |

Among people who read the same recommended books, some are transformed within half a year and some do not change.

The same long instruction goes from necessary to superfluous when the model generation changes.

## Measure the distance first

Before teaching, judge the distance.

| Distance | What to do |
|---|---|
| One step away | Give one principle, or one example |
| Far away | Do not teach. Give an example to copy, or write the judgment into structure |

Explaining again and again to a receiver that is far away wears down the one explaining.

## This book

It is written for those one step away.
