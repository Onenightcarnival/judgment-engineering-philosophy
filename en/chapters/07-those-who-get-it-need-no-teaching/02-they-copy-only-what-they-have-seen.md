# They Copy Only What They Have Seen

Whoever does not know what good looks like copies the only thing they have seen.

## The same for people and agents

| | What gets copied |
|---|---|
| A person | Code already in the repository, the way the last project did it |
| An agent | Code in its context, common patterns in its training data |

## Two corollaries

- Examples set the ceiling. The best thing a receiver has seen is the ceiling of what it produces.
- Bad spreads as fast as good. Once a poor piece of code is merged, it becomes the next thing copied.

Gatekeeping guards the stock of examples.

## What to do

Supply examples: project templates, an SDK, sample design documents.

Most people and most agents improve by copying examples. This is the main channel of transmission.

## Two carriers

| | Principles | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Form | A few sentences | A finished piece that can be copied |
| What it asks of the receiver | Much | Little |
| How far it reaches | Wide | As far as the examples cover |

## The limit of examples

Where the examples do not reach, the receiver falls back to its original level.
