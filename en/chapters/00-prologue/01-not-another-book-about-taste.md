# Not Another Book About Taste

## Books about taste usually do three things

- Show good things.
- Praise people who have taste.
- Urge the reader to improve their taste.

## What this book does not do

- It does not list good designs to be admired.
- It does not offer steps for improving taste.
- It does not argue both sides.
- It does not answer objections.

## What this book does

- It gives definitions.
- It lays out the order in which judgment forms.
- Every chapter covers both people and agents.

## Definitions

- Discernment is knowing what is worth it.
- Discipline is not wavering on what is worth it.
- Judgment is both present at once.

Judgment, once internalized, is called taste.

## Where taste sits

Taste is where the book ends.

The book starts from judgment.
