# Base Rates Do Not Listen to Reason

A base rate is the historical distribution of outcomes for one kind of undertaking.

## A few base rates

| An active departure from the baseline | The outcome in most cases, historically |
|---|---|
| Picking stocks to beat the index | Loses to it over the long run |
| Building a component to replace the mature one | Abandoned |
| Rewriting from scratch | Late |
| Predicting short-term moves | Worse than not predicting |

## It does not listen to reason

A base rate does not explain the individual case.

Everyone who departs has good reasons. The base rate counts exactly those people with good reasons.

## How to use it

1. Ask for the base rate first.
2. Then look at the case.
3. Without evidence of being an exception, act as a non-exception.

"This time is different" needs evidence. A reason is not evidence.

## For agents

One successful run does not change the base rate.

| What is looked at | What is learned |
|---|---|
| A single result | One case |
| The pass rate of the same task over many runs | The base rate of this configuration |

Evaluate an agent by its pass rate.
