# Become the Baseline Before Trying to Beat It

The first step toward beating the baseline is reaching it.

## Becoming the baseline

| Field | Baseline | Becoming it |
|---|---|---|
| Investing | The market index | Holding the index |
| Python engineering | The community's standard conventions and toolchain | Formatter, linter, type checker all on, all green first |
| Product design | The best product of its kind | First matching it |
| Agents | A strong model with default settings | First measuring how the defaults perform |

## Skipping this step

Trying to beat the baseline without becoming it leaves no way to tell whether a change adds or subtracts.

No reference, no difference.

## Afterwards

Once at the baseline, every departure has a reference.

| | Without a baseline | With a baseline |
|---|---|---|
| The effect of a change | A feeling | The difference from the baseline |
| When the change makes things worse | Unknown | Fall back to the baseline |

## Most of the time, stopping at the baseline is enough

Reaching the baseline already beats the outcome of most active departures.

What remains is holding it.
