# No Use, No Judgment

Nobody can imagine what lies beyond what they know.

The standing to judge a thing comes from having used it.

## Depth of use

| Depth of use | What can be judged |
|---|---|
| Never used it | Nothing |
| Tried it once | Whether it is easy to pick up |
| Used it daily for half a year | Where it flows and where it chafes |
| Shipped with it, got burned by it | Where its limits are, and which parts are worth copying |

Reading reviews, hearing reports and watching demos all stop at the first row.

## The gap

People who have not used a thing discuss it from imagination. Imagination is capped by what they have seen.

The conclusion of a discussion rises no higher than the person in the room who has used the thing most deeply.

When nobody in the room has used it in depth, the discussion produces nothing.

## Closing the gap

There is one way: use it.

## For agents

An agent has "used" something in two senses: it saw it in training, or it actually ran it in the environment.

| Before asking the agent for a judgment | What comes back |
|---|---|
| Give it nothing | The average impression from training data |
| Have it read the source and the docs | A judgment based on what this version actually contains |
| Have it run the thing | A judgment based on actual behavior |

The same holds for accepting an agent's output. Someone who has not used a thing in depth cannot accept what an agent built with it.
