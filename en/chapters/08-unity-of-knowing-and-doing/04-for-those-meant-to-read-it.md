# For Those Meant to Read It

This book does not teach.

Those who get it need no teaching; those who need teaching won't get it.

## What it does

It lays out the order in which judgment forms: deference, discipline, discernment, derivation, transmission, unity.

## What is read

| Reader | What they read |
|---|---|
| Already on this path | Which step they are at |
| Not yet on it | Some simple principles |

## Verification

The judgments in this book, by the book's own standard, are left to time.
