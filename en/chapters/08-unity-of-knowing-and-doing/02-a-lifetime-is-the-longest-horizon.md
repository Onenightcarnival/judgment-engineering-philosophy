# A Lifetime Is the Longest Horizon

## The longer the horizon, the fewer things are worth it

| Horizon | What remains |
|---|---|
| A day | Almost everything seems important |
| A year | A few things |
| A lifetime | Very few |

## The longest horizon, the simplest practice

1. Settle on the very few things worth it.
2. Do them with simple methods that time has verified.
3. Repeat.
4. Do not be interrupted.

## The cost

- Boredom. The method is simple and each day is the same.
- Solitude. Verification takes a long time, with no feedback in between.

## Generations

| | Lifespan |
|---|---|
| A piece of code | A few years |
| A framework | Ten years or so |
| A generation of models | Shorter |
| A way of making judgments | A lifetime |

Tools change generations. Judgment does not.
